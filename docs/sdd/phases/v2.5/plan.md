- **D-821 — One pointer handler on every box: the second pointer ends the drag and makes the pinch; the finger left after it does not drag.**
  (R125; FR-SKY-14, FR-STAR-4 as amended; D-798, D-750.) `SkyWindow` keeps a map of the active pointers.
  One pointer is FR-STAR-4's drag, under the hand only; the second makes the two a pinch — the drag ends where it is and its pending move is dropped, so the picture does not jump as the second finger lands — and a third is nothing.
  This supersedes R113's "a second pointer while the first is down is ignored, and so is its lift" (`SkyWindow.test.tsx`'s case is rewritten).
  The pinch's factor is the product of the two pointers' distance before over after since the last frame (fingers apart narrow the field), multiplied into one pending factor that ctrl + wheel and the keys share, and applied in the drag's animation frame: one zoom per frame whatever drove it, about the centre, the aim untouched.
  When one finger lifts the pinch is over and the finger left does not take up the drag: a jump to where it now stands would be the first thing the reader saw; a new finger drags again.
  The pointer handlers are on every box, the sensor's too (the screen's pinch), and `touch-action: none` moves from the hand's class to `.box` so the browser's own pinch is off over the notes as well as the drawing; `tabIndex`, the keys and `data-dragging` stay the hand's.
  The `wheel` listener is native and `passive: false` — React registers `wheel` passive, and a passive listener cannot keep the browser from zooming the page — calling `preventDefault` only with `ctrlKey`; `+`/`=`/`-`/`0` with ctrl or meta are left to the browser (FR-SKY-14: the browser's own ctrl + `+` untouched), and the arrows turn with any modifier as before.
  A pointer is forgotten when it leaves the box uncaptured and when a new primary pointer of its type lands (every earlier one has lifted), so a capture that failed cannot leave a ghost finger that makes the next touch a pinch; two fingers that land on one point pinch from where they first part.
  The hover's label stays the hand's.
  The drag's 4 px per degree is the dome's at every field, so a 20° field drags more of itself per pixel; that is what "zoom changes the projection's scale and nothing else" means for the hand.
