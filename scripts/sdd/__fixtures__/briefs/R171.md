## Decisions (PLAN.md)

- **D-178 — The bundle budgets are re-set to the measured build plus a tenth, not left at the phase's ceilings.** SPEC §9 Phase 2 asks for "the budgets in PLAN §11 re-set for the new chunks", and §11's v1 numbers were written before the chunks existed: they are the room the phase was allowed to take, and as a regression check they are close to useless — the worker sat at 36.1 KB under a 120 KB budget, so a change that tripled it would still have printed `ok`. Each budget is now `measured × 1.1`, rounded up to the next 5 KB, floored at 10 KB (below that the rounding is noise), and capped at the §11 ceiling: main 150, chart 110, worker 40, astronomy 25, live 10, service worker 10. Chart is the one that lands on its ceiling, at 97.1 KB against 110 — D-63 already says the 60 KB planned before the R14 spike is not reachable from outside glyphcss. The two numbers keep different jobs: §11's ceiling is what a feature may claim without an amendment, and the budget in the script is what the build log complains about, so a stray dependency shows up as a `::warning::` on the PR that added it rather than a year later. The script still never fails the build (§11), so a legitimate overrun costs an annotation and a line in the PR, and the fix is to re-measure and re-set — not to widen the number and move on.

- **D-1042 — Strings, tests and captures.** (FR-GLOBE-16, FR-GLOBE-18.) A `globe` section in `src/i18n/{en,es}/` holds every word of §4.55 (in `main` by FR-I18N-2's one catalog, within `GLOBE_MAIN_KB`). Tests: `globe/{projection,scene}.test.ts`, `GlobeRenderer.test.ts` (jsdom: node counts, a frame's attributes, the deep stars' append), `SkyGlobe.test.tsx` (keys, wheel, the label's naming), `Stars.test.tsx`'s wide globe cases (the view row, the rail's groups, the second playback, the places, the faint toggle), `tests/e2e/globe.spec.ts` (1280 × 800 and 1920 × 1080: the breakpoint crossing, drag, keys, steps, ×3600 under a paused `page.clock`, a saved place, Tab order, the hash unchanged), `tests/e2e/globe-perf.spec.ts` (`stars-perf.spec.ts`'s CDP throttle at 4×, best of three drags and a ×3600 run, faint off and on), `axe.spec.ts` with the globe shown. `captureSeeds.ts` gains a London favourite; `captureSet.ts` the five screens of FR-GLOBE-18 and `stars-whole`'s widths `[390]`.

- **D-1052 — The release's capture diff is measured on one machine, and only what moved is committed.** (FR-GLOBE-18, FR-CAP-5; D-742; R171.) A headless task cannot dispatch `captures.yml` (no `gh`), so R171 measured as R161 did: 2.11.0's set shot by `8413d978`'s own spec off a plain build of `8413d978` extracted into a scratch tree, and the branch's set shot twice off the branch's plain build, all on one Mac. The comparison is between those runs, never against the committed Linux raster, which differs from a Mac's everywhere. What is committed is 2.11.0's files where nothing moved, the Mac run's files for the 12 moved (the wide view row's word) and the 24 new, and the four `stars-whole-1280` files deleted; `captures.yml`'s run on `main` after the merge is the set of record, and whether its artefact replaces the mixed set is the owner's (RELEASE §25.4). The routes: `globeShown` ticks the paused clock until the zenith's mark and the rail's groups are drawn (the two lazy chunks' Suspense reveals, R32) and pins `CLOCK`, which is the globe's own instant too (its playback opens at now); `stars-globe-place` scrolls London's row to the rail's end, since the click's own scroll differs by where the row starts; `stars-globe-named` pins first and presses the row after, since `STAR_LABEL_HOLD_S` is 4 s and a pin is a 10 s tick. The London favourite is `observers.ts`'s geocoded `LONDON`, not `globeHelpers.ts`'s literal, so the rail's name is the geocoder's (`London, England, United Kingdom`).

- **D-1053 — `live` and `window` give bytes back to shared chunks, and that is not a moved row.** (FR-GLOBE-15, D-178; R171.) Against 2.11.0, `live` is −1,074 bytes and `window` −135: what the globe shares with them (`shownTime`, `useWallThrottle`, `skySurface`, `starGlyphs.module`, `deepChunk`) is a chunk of its own now. FR-GLOBE-15's "no other row moves" is read as "no other row grows": no budget changes, and RELEASE §25.4 asks the owner to accept the reading. `globe` leaves `PHASE_BUDGETS` for `BUDGETS` at 15 (13.5 × 1.1 = 14.9, GLOBE_BUDGET_KB its ceiling).

- **D-89 — The turn cap is 250, and a headless session commits as it goes.** R17, the language task, ended at turn 121 of 120 with the `src/i18n` catalogs written, some thirty files rewired through them, the co-located tests not yet migrated to the new signatures, and **nothing committed** — so the driver's step-4 check (has the branch any commits?) found an empty branch and scored the whole run lost. Two things were wrong. The cap was the binding constraint at 40 % of the wall clock: 121 turns took 18.6 minutes, about 9 s a turn, so 120 turns could never use the 45 minutes §16.4 allows; 250 turns is roughly 38 minutes, which puts the two limits at the same place and leaves the clock as the backstop it was meant to be. And a session that commits once at the end converts *any* limit into total loss, which is the opposite of what §16.5 intends by keeping the branch and the worktree. `sdd-implement` already said to commit in small steps; the driver's prompt now says it too, and the skill says why: on a branch nobody has pushed, a red typecheck between steps costs nothing and an uncommitted working tree costs the whole session. What this does not fix is a task that is simply too large for one session; that is a breakdown question, and R17's retry is the measurement.
